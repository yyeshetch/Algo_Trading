Buyer's Day Meter

—

Score trend (every 5 min) — green ≥60 buy · yellow 40-59 mixed · red <40 avoid

Buy (either side) when green: range wide, trend efficiency & ADX high, few VWAP crosses, long straddle holding/expanding — movement beats theta.
Stand aside when red: tight range, choppy ER, many VWAP crosses, low ADX, straddle bleeding — theta day, buyers lose.

Long ATM Straddle — Realized vs Implied Vol

Implied = straddle ÷ spot (market-priced move %) · Realized = session range % + rolling 30-min vol scaled to session

Expansion (long straddle wins): realized / rolling RV crosses above implied — movement beats what was priced; straddle P&L % rising.
Contraction (theta day): realized range stays below open implied and straddle bleeds — avoid buying premium; stand aside or scalp only.
